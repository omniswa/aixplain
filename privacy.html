Privacy policy

aixplain is a simple reading site. We don’t ask you to sign up and we don’t sell your personal information. The site does show ads from third parties, which use cookies. Here is exactly what that means.

Last updated Nov 26, 2026 | 4 min read

The short version

  • No accounts, no sign-up forms, and no comments on this site.
  • The site shows ads served by Google AdSense and other ad networks. These partners may use cookies or similar technology on your device.
  • Where the law requires it, such as in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, ad cookies are only used after you agree.
  • You can change your ad choices at any time. See “Your choices” below.
  • One small preference (light or dark theme) is saved on your own device and never sent to us.

Who we are

aixplain (“we”, “us”) is a website that publishes short articles on whatever is trending, run by xclnt-sensai. This policy applies to this website only. If you have a question about it, the contact details are at the end of this page.

What we collect and store

The table below lists everything that happens to your data when you visit aixplain.

Data involved when you use aixplain
Item Where it lives Why
Theme choice Your browser’s local storage only To remember light or dark mode between visits.

What we don’t collect

We don’t ourselves collect your name, email address, or contacts, and we don’t sell or rent personal information. Our ad partners may collect information such as your IP address, device and browser details, and pages you view, as described in the next sections. We don’t receive that data in a form that identifies you.

Cookies and local storage

Our own code doesn’t set cookies. It uses your browser’s local storage to keep one value, theme, set to light or dark after you press the theme button. That value stays on your device.

Our advertising partners and consent tool do use cookies and similar technologies, as explained below. You can remove all of them at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings, and you can change your consent choice using the privacy settings link or button the consent tool provides on the site.

Advertising

Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this site and other sites on the internet. Google’s advertising cookies let it and its partners show you ads based on your browsing history.

You can learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services at Google’s partner sites page.

Other ad networks

We may also work with other advertising networks or vendors. If so, they may place their own cookies or identifiers on your device to serve and measure ads.

Consent in Europe, the UK, and Switzerland

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we ask for your consent through a consent banner before ad cookies or similar storage are used. Without consent, you may see limited or no ads, and no ad cookies are set for personalization.

Personalized and non-personalized ads

Personalized ads use your past activity to choose what you see. Non-personalized ads rely on the page you are reading and your general location, though they may still use cookies for frequency limits, reporting, and fraud prevention.

Hosting and third parties

Hosting

The site is served as static files by a third-party host. When your browser requests a page, the host receives technical information such as your IP address, and may log it under its own privacy policy. We don’t have a way to identify you from those logs.

Telegram

Our home page links to a Telegram group. Once you open that link you are using Telegram, and its terms and privacy policy apply to anything you do there, including what you post and what other members can see.

Links to other sites

Articles often link to outside sources, and ads link to advertisers’ sites. We don’t control those sites and aren’t responsible for how they handle your data.

Sharing an article

The “Share” button uses your device’s share sheet when available, or copies the page link to your clipboard. Nothing is sent to us when you do this.

Children

aixplain is a general-audience site and isn’t directed at children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children, and we don’t knowingly ask ad partners to show personalized ads to them.

Your choices

  • Use the consent banner or privacy settings link on the site to change your ad cookie choice.
  • Turn off personalized ads from Google at Google Ads Settings.
  • Clear this site’s cookies and local storage in your browser, or use a content blocker.
  • Ask us a question about this policy using the contact details below.

Changes to this policy

If we add something that changes how data is handled, such as a new ad network, analytics, or a newsletter, we will update this page and the “last updated” date at the top before it goes live.
