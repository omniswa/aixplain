Privacy policy

aixplain is a simple reading site. We don’t ask you to sign up, we don’t run ads, and we don’t track you across the web. Here is exactly what that means.

Last updated Nov 26, 2026 | 4 min read

The short version

  • No accounts, no sign-up forms, and no comments on this site.
  • No advertising and no analytics or tracking scripts.
  • One small preference (light or dark theme) is saved on your own device and never sent to us.
  • The site is hosted by a third party, which may keep standard server logs.

Who we are

aixplain (“we”, “us”) is a website that publishes short articles on whatever is trending, run by omniswa-ai. This policy applies to this website only. If you have a question about it, the contact details are at the end of this page.

What we collect and store

The table below lists everything that happens to your data when you visit aixplain.

Data involved when you use aixplain
Item Where it lives Why
Theme choice Your browser’s local storage only To remember light or dark mode between visits.
Server logs Our hosting provider Standard technical records, such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested, used to run and secure the host.
Telegram activity Telegram Only if you choose to join our group. We don’t receive your data from the click.

What we don’t collect

We don’t collect your name, email address, location, or contacts. We don’t use advertising identifiers, fingerprinting, or third-party tracking pixels. We don’t sell or rent personal information, because we don’t hold any.

Cookies and local storage

aixplain doesn’t set cookies. It does use your browser’s local storage to keep one value, theme, set to light or dark after you press the theme button. That value stays on your device. If you never press the button, nothing is stored and the site follows your system setting.

You can remove the saved value at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings.

Hosting and third parties

Hosting

The site is served as static files by a third-party host. When your browser requests a page, the host receives technical information such as your IP address, and may log it under its own privacy policy. We don’t have a way to identify you from those logs.

Telegram

Our home page links to a Telegram group. Once you open that link you are using Telegram, and its terms and privacy policy apply to anything you do there, including what you post and what other members can see.

Links to other sites

Articles often link to outside sources. We don’t control those sites and aren’t responsible for how they handle your data. External links open with noopener so the other site can’t access this one.

Sharing an article

The “Share” button uses your device’s share sheet when available, or copies the page link to your clipboard. Nothing is sent to us when you do this.

Children

aixplain is a general-audience site and isn’t directed at children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect information from anyone, children included.

Your choices

  • Clear this site’s local storage to remove your theme choice.
  • Use your browser’s privacy settings or a content blocker if you want extra control over what any site can store.
  • Ask us a question about this policy using the contact details below.

Changes to this policy

If we add something that changes how data is handled, such as analytics or a newsletter, we will update this page and the “last updated” date at the top before it goes live.
